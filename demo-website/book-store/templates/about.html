About PageTurner Books

Our Story

PageTurner Books was founded in 2011 by two lifelong readers who believed that great literature should be easy to find, fairly priced, and beautifully presented. What started as a small independent shop in Portland, Oregon has grown into a curated online catalogue serving readers across more than 40 countries.

We specialise in classic and literary fiction — the books that have shaped how people think, feel, and see the world. Every title in our catalogue is hand-picked by our editorial team and comes with a personal recommendation written by a human, not an algorithm.

What We Believe

Books are not commodities. We refuse to compete on volume or price alone. Instead we focus on quality — quality of selection, quality of presentation, and quality of service. Every order is packed by hand and ships with a short note about the book inside.

We also believe in paying authors and publishers fairly. We do not discount below recommended retail price, and we work directly with independent publishers wherever possible.

Our Team

We are a team of twelve, based in Portland. Our buyers, writers, and customer service staff are all avid readers. If you email us with a question about a book, you will get a reply from someone who has actually read it.

Sustainability

All our packaging is plastic-free and made from recycled materials. We offset the carbon footprint of every delivery through a verified reforestation programme. We are working towards becoming a fully carbon-neutral business by 2027.
